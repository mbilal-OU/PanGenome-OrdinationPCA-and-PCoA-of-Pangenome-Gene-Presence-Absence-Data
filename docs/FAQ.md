# FAQ

## Does PCA replace Roary summary?

No. Roary summary and PCA answer different questions.

## Are top-loading genes responsible for adaptation?

Not automatically. They are candidate genes contributing to PCA separation.

## Why remove zero-variance genes?

Genes present in all genomes or absent in all genomes cannot separate genomes. PCA requires variation.

## Should I use PCA or PCoA?

Use both if possible. PCA gives gene loadings. Jaccard PCoA is distance-based and well suited to binary presence/absence data.
